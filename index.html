Stays

Busan · 16–18 Jan · 2 nights

Lavalse Hotel

82 Bongnaenaru-ro, Yeongdo-gu, Busan. At the foot of Yeongdo Bridge, about 10 minutes' walk from Nampo Station (Line 1).

  • Room: Ondol Ocean View, 3 futons (traditional heated floor)
  • Check-in 15:00 · check-out 11:00. Ask to drop bags early on arrival.
  • Walk to Jagalchi Market, BIFF Square and Nampo-dong
Seoul · 18–23 Jan · 5 nights

Hongdae Airbnb

5 Wausan-ro 29ma-gil, Mapo-gu, Seoul. In the Hongdae area, near Hongik Univ. Station (Line 2, AREX and Gyeongui–Jungang Line).

  • AREX goes straight from Seoul Station and Incheon Airport
  • Busking, cafés and late-night food on your doorstep

Busan Must-Sees

Five stops. Two days.

Gamcheon is close to the hotel. The other four are on the east side, so they're grouped into one day.

01
West · Day 1

Gamcheon Culture Village

Pastel hillside houses, murals and alleys. About 30 minutes by bus from the hotel area.

02
East · Day 2

Haedong Yonggungsa

A Buddhist temple built on the rocks by the sea. Go early to beat the crowds.

03
East · Day 2

Skyline Luge

Gravity karts in Osiria, close to Yonggungsa. Buy a multi-ride pass.

04
East · Day 2

Haeundae Sky Capsule

Tiny coloured pods along the coastal rail between Mipo and Cheongsapo. Book a slot ahead.

05
East · Day 2

Shinsegae Centum City

A huge department store with Spa Land inside. Good place to warm up in the evening.

What to Wear

Dress in layers. Seoul in January is properly cold.

Typical January temperatures are below. Seoul often goes well below zero, and wind makes it feel colder. Check the forecast a week before you fly.

Busan · 16–18 Jan
~7°high~-1°low

Milder but windy by the sea, especially at Yonggungsa and on the Sky Capsule line.

Seoul · 18–23 Jan
~1°high~-7°low

Dry and freezing. Cold snaps can drop below -10°C.

01

Base layer

Thermal top and leggings (Heattech or similar). Wear them every day.

02

Mid layer

A knit sweater or fleece. Indoors is heated, so it should come off easily.

03

Outer layer

A long padded coat that's windproof and covers your hips.

04

Extremities

Beanie, scarf, touchscreen gloves, warm socks and comfy waterproof shoes.

Getting Around

One card, three apps

01

T-money

A tap card for subways and buses in both cities. Buy and top it up at convenience stores.

02

Naver Map

Google Maps directions are limited in Korea, so use Naver or KakaoMap for transit.

03

Kakao T

For taxis, especially with luggage or late at night.

04

Papago

Translates menus and signs with your camera.

Notes

The fine print

Handy phrases

  • Annyeonghaseyo (안녕하세요): Hello
  • Kamsahamnida (감사합니다): Thank you
  • Igeo juseyo (이거 주세요): This one, please
  • Eolmayeyo? (얼마예요?): How much?
  • Hwajangsil eodiyeyo? (화장실 어디예요?): Where's the toilet?

Good to know

  • Korea is SGT + 1 hour.
  • Emergency: 112 for police, 119 for fire and ambulance. Tourist helpline: 1330.
  • Ondol rooms have heated floors, so sleeping on futons is normal.
  • Booking references are kept off this page on purpose. Find them in your apps.
